# PLZ-2026 index update — 15 September 2026

The published AHSY PLZ indices were regenerated from the 2026 microdata with
the Python implementation maintained in the production toolkit on the A drive.
The previous published residential, office, and retail files are retained in
`OLD/` for reference.

This release contains residential rent, residential purchase, office rent,
retail rent, and gastro-retail rent indices for 8,255 postcode areas and the
years 2007–2025. Estimates require at least 50 transactions from the target
year within the selected spatial estimation window. Estimates failing that
threshold are excluded and the level index (`price_qm`) is imputed from
adjacent years. The underlying log estimate and its standard error remain
missing so that imputed observations can be identified.

For retail and gastro-retail, 2007, 2008, 2019, 2020, and 2021 remain
suppressed in accordance with the established application definitions.

Each product is supplied in long and wide form as both CSV and Stata DTA.
